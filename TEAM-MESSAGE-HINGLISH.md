*Sabse badi baat: ab likhna nahi padega* ✍️❌
Pehle har cheez type karni padti thi. Ab zyada tar sawaalon me *list khulegi, usme se ek
option choose kar dijiye*. Bas tap tap tap. Ek society 1 minute me ho jayegi.

*Kya kya poochha jaayega:*
1️⃣ *Maintenance* — kitna hai (list me se), aur kaun leta hai (RWA / builder / koi nahi)
2️⃣ *RWA* — registered hai, sirf committee hai, ya nahi bani
3️⃣ *Transfer / NOC* — kitna time lagta hai, aur charge kitna
4️⃣ *Bank* — jo bank loan dete hain unpar tap kar dijiye (ek se zyada chun sakte ho)
5️⃣ *Services* — paani, bijli backup, lift (flat me) ya sadak, sewer, bijli (plot me)
6️⃣ *Club house* ya *handover* — society kis type ki hai uske hisaab se

Ye 6 cheezein kisi portal par nahi milti — na 99acres, na Magicbricks. Yahi hamari sabse
badi taakat hai, kyunki ye sirf humein pata hai.

*"Pata nahi" ab sabse pehla option hai* 👍
Har list me sabse upar *"pata nahi"* likha hoga. Jo cheez confirm nahi hai, uspar seedha
"pata nahi" choose kar dijiye. Ye galti *nahi* hai. Website par hum saaf likhte hain ki ye
humne confirm nahi kiya, aur customer ko wo aur acha lagta hai.

*Andaaza mat lagaiye.* Ek galat baat website par gayi to customer ka bharosa jaata hai, aur
wo wapas nahi aata.

*Agar list me aapka jawab nahi hai:*
Kuch list me sabse neeche *"koi aur"* option hai. Wo choose karoge to ek box khulega, usme
likh dijiye. Jaise maintenance ₹2.25 hai to "koi aur" me likh dijiye.

*Kaise use karna hai:*
1️⃣ Link kholiye, saari societies list me dikhengi
2️⃣ Jis par kaam karna hai uspar *tap* kariye, wo khul jayegi
3️⃣ Agar aap khud gaye the to *"main khud gaya tha"* tap kar dijiye. Ye zaroori hai, isse
website par "humne khud dekha" likha ja sakta hai
4️⃣ Har sawaal me se option choose kariye
5️⃣ Society poori hote hi card *green* ho jayega aur ✅ lag jayega
6️⃣ 3-4 societies ho jayen to neeche *green Submit* dabaiye

*Neeche "Extra" wala hissa* 📋
Har society me neeche *"Extra (zaroori nahi)"* likha ek hissa hai — possession kab mili,
abhi kuch bikne ko hai ya nahi, jagah kaunsi hai, kiraya kitna. *Ye bharna zaroori nahi
hai*, bina bhare bhi society poori ginti hai. Jab time mile tab bhar dijiye, website par
societies ka comparison table isi se banta hai.

*Submit baar baar dabana padega* 🔁
Har baar sirf *nayi puri hui* societies jaati hain, purani dobara nahi. Saari societies ek
saath nahi ja sakti kyunki WhatsApp lamba message kaat deta hai. Tool khud hi tukdon me
bhejta hai aur har message par *"part 1"*, *"part 2"* likh deta hai.

*Tool khud yaad dilayega* 🔔
Agar koi society bhejni baaki reh gayi, to upar *peela warning* dikhega —
*"⚠️ 12 society bhejni baaki hai"* — aur neeche wala *green button peela ho jayega* aur us
par likha hoga *"Submit — 12 baaki hai"*.

*Jab tak wo peela dikh raha hai, kaam poora nahi hua.* Submit dobara dabate rahiye jab tak
wo hat na jaye. WhatsApp band karke wapas aaoge tab bhi wo peela wahi rahega, isliye bhoolne
ka darr nahi hai.

⚠️ *Har message ke aakhir me "khatam, 4 society" likha hona chahiye.* Agar wo line nahi
