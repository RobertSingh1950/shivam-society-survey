1️⃣ *Maintenance* — kitna hai, aur kaun leta hai (builder / RWA / koi nahi)

3️⃣ *Transfer / NOC* — kitne din lagte hain, kitna charge hai, koi shart hai kya
4️⃣ *Bank loan* — kaun se bank yahan loan dete hain, koi mana karta hai kya

Aur ek aur cheez jo bahut kaam ki hai:
5️⃣ *Comparison* — customer is society ko kis doosri society se compare karta hai. Jaise
"Essentia dekhun ya MVL Coral?" Jo jodi baar baar aati hai, wahi humein website par likhni
chahiye.
