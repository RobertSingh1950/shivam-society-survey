📖 Ye tool kaise use karna hai (ek baar padh lijiye)

Har society ke baare mein humein 4 cheezein chahiye. Ye 4 cheezein kisi bhi portal par nahi milti, isliye website par ye hamari sabse badi taakat banengi. Buyer inhi 4 par phasta hai.

  • Ek baar mein ek society bhariye. 5 minute ka kaam hai.
  • Jo pata na ho, uspar "pata nahi" daba dijiye. Andaaza mat lagaiye.
  • Agar aap khud society gaye the to "main khud gaya tha" tick kariye. Isse website par "humne khud dekha" likha ja sakta hai, aur wahi sabse zyada bharosa banati hai.
  • Neeche WhatsApp button dabaiye, message bana banaya khul jayega, bas send kar dijiye.

Ek zaroori baat: is tool mein kisi client ka naam, number, ya kisi seller ka rate kabhi mat likhiye. Ye sirf buildings ke baare mein hai, logon ke baare mein nahi.

"Pata nahi" bharna galti nahi hai. Website par hum likhte hain ki ye cheez humne confirm nahi ki. Galat number likhne se bharosa jaata hai, aur wapas nahi aata.

Andaaza kabhi nahi. Jo confirm nahi hai, uspar "pata nahi" daba dijiye. Ek galat number website par gaya to customer ka bharosa jaata hai, aur usko wapas laana mushkil hai. Khali jagah chhodna bilkul theek hai.
